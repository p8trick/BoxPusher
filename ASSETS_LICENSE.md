## Kenney (CC0)

Fonts, board tiles, and most icons come from Kenney free asset packs under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
(public domain dedication; commercial and non-commercial use allowed; credit not required).

Includes:

- `assets/Player/`, `Ground/`, `Blocks/`, `Crates/` (Kenney Sokoban Pack and similar)
- `assets/fonts/` (Kenney Future / Future Narrow)
- most icons in `assets/UI/dpad/` and `assets/UI/icons/`
- PWA icons in `assets/icons/` (derived from the character face art; same CC0)

Filenames were renamed for this project and may differ from Kenney’s originals.  
Source: https://kenney.nl/ — thanks, Kenney.

## Other UI art (AI-generated) / 其余 UI 图（生成图）

Wood frames, plank buttons, level-select tiles, home backgrounds, and the win-panel
plate were generated by AI tools. They are **not** Kenney files. The exact models
were not recorded.

Do not label these as Kenney or as a CC0 asset pack. Personal / family use.
If the project is published later, re-check the terms of the tools used at that time.

木框、木板按钮、选关木板、主页背景、过关底板等其余界面图为 AI 生成，
不是 Kenney 包内文件。具体生成工具未逐一记录。

不要标成 Kenney，也不要标成 CC0 素材包。家人/个人使用。
